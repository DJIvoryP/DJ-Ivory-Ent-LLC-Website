Event Recap

5th Annual Back-to-School Summit

We had the pleasure of providing entertainment for Amplify Community Resources and Greater Miami Service Corps' back-to-school summit.

This year's theme, "Connect & Capable," focuses on equipping students and families with the resources, connections, and emerging technology knowledge — including artificial intelligence (AI) — needed to thrive in school and beyond.

Hosted By
Amplify Community Resources & Greater Miami Service Corps
Date
Wednesday, July 29, 2026
Time
9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
Location
Miami Dade College Medical Campus, Miami, FL
Music By
DJ Ivory P

Attendees had the chance to win raffle prizes including a Miami Dolphins autographed jersey and iPads. There was a book giveaway, and students not only had the opportunity to check out an AI and robotics simulation but bookbags and supplies were provided for every student who attended.

Sponsors
City of Miami Police Department The Children's Trust Amazon WISH Foundation, Inc. Educational Federal Credit Union
Partners
Miami Dade College The Children's Trust Books for Free Hope for Miami
