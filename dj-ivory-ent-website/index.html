About the Company

Founder

DJ Ivory P

Ivory Lewis · Miami, FL

Built DJ Ivory Ent LLC to give Hosts and DJs a real booking and management home — and to give clients one place to find the right talent for the room they're throwing.

Booking & management, not promotion

DJ Ivory Ent LLC is a booking and management company for Hosts and DJs — we schedule the talent, not the event itself. Whether you're planning a private celebration, a corporate stage, a community gathering, or need a resident for your club, we match the room with the right person on the mic or behind the decks.

Every Host and DJ in our roster is vetted and managed directly through us, so clients get one point of contact and consistent, professional coverage from first call to final song.
